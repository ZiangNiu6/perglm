Simulation design

Each cell uses 1000 independent datasets, a two-sided test, nominal level 0.05, and 499 independently sampled permutations. Study-specific fitting controls are epsilon = 1e-10 and maxit = 100, with alternative = "two.sided", batch_size = 64, and keep_statistics = FALSE. This uses a tighter convergence tolerance and a larger iteration limit than the public defaults; the study evaluates this specified configuration. There were 0 errors across the 20,000 regular-null/power datasets; the 912 sparse-exposure failures are reported separately below. Two bounded nuisance covariates U and V are independent Uniform[-1,1]. Ordinary exposures have prevalence 0.3 under marginal independence (MI), or Bernoulli probability expit(logit(0.3) + 1.2U - 0.8V) in confounded conditional-mean (CM) cells. The fitted null is always y ~ u + v.

Gaussian means are 0.4 + 0.6U - 0.4V + gamma X; Poisson log means are 0.2 + 0.5U - 0.3V + gamma X. MI cases add an omitted 0.6U^2 term while keeping X independent of (Y,U,V). CM cases retain the correct fitted null mean. The Gaussian heteroskedastic cell uses SD = exp(0.5U + 0.3X): its conditional mean null holds, while conditional independence is false because variance depends on X. The Poisson variance-misspecification cell generates negative-binomial outcomes with size 2 and the correct Poisson working mean.

Gaussian power coefficients are 0.25 and 0.5; Poisson coefficients are 0.3 and 0.6. Sample sizes are 100 and 500. Separate sparse-exposure stress cases use n = 40 and independent X ~ Bernoulli(0.02).
